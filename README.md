# Content Hub

An AI video studio and posting hub that runs a brand's organic social channels end to end.

- **Makes the videos:** an AI presenter, cloned voice and generated scenes, held to a per-video budget.
- **Schedules and posts** to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok after a human approves each one.
- **Tracks results:** views, followers, cost per video and the calls each video brings in, on one dashboard.
- **Writes better hooks:** a hook rater learns from the team's ratings which openings to make next.

*Built with Next.js, SQLite, Python, ElevenLabs, Seedance and the platform APIs. Source is private.*
